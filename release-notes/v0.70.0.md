## A new Containers page

- **What runs, first:** **Running**, **Stopped** and **All** sit right under the title, with their counts. The page opens on **Running** and remembers your choice.
- **Stacks or one list:** **Stacks** groups containers in a card per stack; **List** shows them all together, running ones first, each with its stack's name.
- **A stack at a glance:** each card has a bar with a segment per container (green when it runs, red when its health check fails, grey when it's stopped) and says "All 8 running" or "7 of 8 running". Stacks with nothing running are dimmed.
- **Rows that read well:** the image under the name, a status from the health check (**Healthy**, **Unhealthy**, **Starting**), a live CPU graph, memory and network. Stopped containers are dimmed, and checkboxes and extra buttons show as you point at a row.
- **Long port lists:** a row shows up to two ports on this computer, the ones that open in the browser first. **+N** lists them all, with the container port each one leads to.
- **Calmer buttons:** **Logs**, **Stop all** and **Open folder** stay out of the way, and **Run** stands out.
